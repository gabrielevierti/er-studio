## SDK reference

It loads as soon as you open it, and follows your cursor: put the caret on a symbol in a `.ts` or `.js` file and the reference jumps to that entry.

Matching is forgiving — `glasses.sendText` finds `sendText`, and case differences still land.

Untick **follow cursor** in the panel to browse without it jumping, and **RELOAD** re-reads the installed SDK after you change project or reinstall packages.
